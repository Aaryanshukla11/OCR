1. WHAT IS THIS COOL PROJECT?
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Imagine you take a picture of a store receipt, a flight ticket, or a school form with your phone. 
To a regular computer, that photo is just a huge grid of colored dots (pixels). The computer has NO IDEA what is written on it!

This project gives the computer TWO superpowers:
  1. 👁️ A Super Robot Eye (OCR): Reads every letter and number inside the picture.
  2. 🧠 A Super Detective Brain (Document Intelligence): Understands what every word and number ACTUALLY means!

Instead of just saying "I see text", the computer can now say:
  "Aha! This paper is an Invoice. The person's name is Aryan Kumar, their phone number is 9810929812, and the total cost was $50.00!"


--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
2. HOW IT WORKS: THE 7 MAGIC STEPS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Step 1: THE SCANNER EYE 👁️ (Text Detection)
  - What it does: Scans the photo or PDF page, finds every piece of text, draws invisible boxes around them, and turns picture letters into real computer text.
  - Tech used: PaddleOCR & OpenCV (Computer Vision tools)
  - Folder location: `backend/app/ocr/`

Step 2: THE DETECTIVE MAP-MAKER 🗺️ (Layout & Grouping)
  - What it does: Figures out how the paper is designed. It groups words into lines and blocks, figuring out reading order (top to bottom, left to right) and separating Headers, Body, and Footers.
  - Folder location: `backend/app/document_intelligence/layout/` & `grouping/`

Step 3: THE MATCHMAKER 🔗 (Key-Value Pairing)
  - What it does: Connects labels to their values! Like matching "Name:" (the Key) on the left with "Aryan Kumar" (the Value) on the right.
  - Folder location: `backend/app/document_intelligence/relationships/`

Step 4: THE GRID MASTER 📊 (Table Detection)
  - What it does: Finds tables! If you bought 5 items at a grocery store, it organizes them nicely into rows and columns so prices don't get mixed up.
  - Folder location: `backend/app/document_intelligence/tables/`

Step 5: THE MEANING TRANSLATOR 🕵️‍♂️ (Semantic Field Identification)
  - What it does: Solves the mystery of what data REALLY represents!
      - If it sees "Ph." or "Mobile: 9810929812" -> It says: "Identified As: Contact Number!"
      - If it sees "Aryan Kumar" -> It says: "Identified As: Person Name!"
      - If it sees "GSTIN: 07AAAAA0000A1Z5" -> It says: "Identified As: Tax ID!"
  - Tech used: Ollama + Qwen AI Model (Local Artificial Intelligence) + Smart Rule Generators
  - Folder location: `backend/app/document_intelligence/semantics/`

Step 6: THE VAULT 🏦 (Database & Search)
  - What it does: Saves all the structured information into a neat digital library so you can search for documents instantly!
  - Tech used: SQLite Database
  - Folder location: `backend/app/services/database.py`

Step 7: THE CONTROL DASHBOARD 🎮 (Web Interface)
  - What it does: Shows a glowing, interactive web app on your screen! You can drag and drop images, see bounding boxes over text, view a 4-column table (Field | Extracted Data | Identified As | Confidence), and inspect developer debug details.
  - Tech used: React, TypeScript, Vite, Tailwind CSS
  - Folder location: `frontend/`
